# Third-Party Notices & Open Source Acknowledgments

VieLotusIM (Sen Việt) utilizes and references several open-source technologies, libraries, and foundational research in Vietnamese computational linguistics:

---

## 1. Bamboo Engine & OpenKey Reference Architectures

- **Bamboo Engine**: [https://github.com/BambooEngine/bamboo-core](https://github.com/BambooEngine/bamboo-core)
- **OpenKey**: [https://github.com/tuyenvm/OpenKey](https://github.com/tuyenvm/OpenKey)
- **UniKey**: Open-source core algorithms by Phạm Kim Long.
- **Description**: Structural references for modern vs traditional diacritic placement, tone placement algorithms, and dictionary-assisted bilingual disambiguation heuristics.
